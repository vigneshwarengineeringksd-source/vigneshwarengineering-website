ABOUT US

Vigneshwar Engineering Industrial

Vigneshwar Engineering Industrial is a trusted supplier of agricultural machinery, laterite stone cutting machines, genuine spare parts, and industrial products based in Kasaragod, Kerala.

We deal in Chinese Power Tillers, I-Beam Type Laterite Stone Cutting Machines, and a wide catalog of spare components, bearings, lubricants, industrial belts, chains, oil seals, and hardware fasteners.

Our focus is on delivering verified product reliability, competitive pricing, and technical support to farmers, machine operators, quarry owners, and service workshops.
